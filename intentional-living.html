A room does not need to look like a showroom to support the life happening inside it. This visual essay uses four illustrative photographs to explore space, light and the things we choose to keep.

Less to manage, more to notice

Start with the place where your day begins. What do you reach for? What do you move out of the way? A useful space makes ordinary actions easier. The photographs below are visual references, not a documented tour of one home.

Four studies in attention

Select a photograph to view the gallery.

One useful edit

Choose one corner, drawer or daily routine. Remove what repeatedly gets in the way. Put one useful thing where it belongs. Live with the change for a week before buying a solution.

A well-used life will leave things out of place. Make room for that, too.
