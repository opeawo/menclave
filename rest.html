Make Room for Rest

An invitation to give recovery a place in your week.

MENCLAVE · EDITORIAL CONCEPT

← All stories

Notice your rhythm

Consider which parts of the week leave you depleted and which help you feel present. Rest begins with paying attention to those patterns.

Protect a pause

Set aside a manageable stretch of time without a productivity goal. A quiet walk, a shared meal or an evening away from work can be a starting point.

Let others help

Talk about competing responsibilities with the people who share them. A sustainable rhythm often requires cooperation.
