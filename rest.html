Make Room for Rest

An invitation to give recovery a place in your week.

MENCLAVE · EDITORIAL CONCEPT

ON THIS PAGE

A minute without an outcomeLet one task waitReturn gently

Before turning rest into another assignment, try giving your attention to one quiet thing. This page demonstrates a video-led feature with a playable clip, a description and a short companion practice.

A minute without an outcome

Sit somewhere comfortable. Put your phone out of reach if you can. Notice what your attention moves towards when nothing is asking for a response. You do not have to clear your mind or produce an insight.

01

Let one task wait

Choose something non-urgent and give yourself a definite time to return to it. A boundary is easier to trust when it has a beginning and an end.

02

Return gently

When the pause ends, notice whether you need water, food, company or a quieter evening. Treat the observation as information rather than another target.
