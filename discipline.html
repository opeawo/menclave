A useful routine should fit an ordinary Tuesday. Use this seven-day practice to turn an intention into something you can repeat without turning your whole life into a productivity project.

Before you begin

Choose one practice: ten quiet minutes, a short walk, a page of reading or a call you keep delaying. Set a minimum that still feels possible on a difficult day. Completing the minimum counts.
