FEATURE INTERVIEW

FROM AMBITION
TO SURRENDER

A conversation about faith, leadership and the freedom that comes when you stop trying to build a kingdom of your own.

FICTIONAL EDITORIAL SAMPLE · INTERVIEW FORMAT PREVIEW

He had built a life that looked successful from the outside. But behind the ambitions and the expectations was a deeper question: what does success matter if it costs you your peace?

In this fictional editorial demonstration, we explore ambition, responsibility, leadership and the role of faith in a meaningful life.

You've been open about a season in your life when ambition was driving everything. What changed?

I looked successful from the outside: long hours, big goals and constant momentum. But I was restless and never satisfied. I began questioning what I was building and why.

How has faith shaped the way you lead, both at work and at home?

It reminds me that leadership isn't about control or personal gain. It's about stewardship, service and the people whose lives are affected by our choices.

“I realized I had built a life around achievement instead of purpose.”

What do you see as the greatest responsibility of a man today?

To lead with character, to be present for the people who depend on us, and to use whatever influence we have to help others thrive.

What would you say to a man torn between ambition and faith?

Ambition can be meaningful. The question is what it serves. Pursue worthy goals, but don't make success your entire identity.
