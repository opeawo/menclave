What Does It Really Mean to Be a Good Man?

Character is formed in the choices that rarely make the headlines.

MENCLAVE · EDITORIAL CONCEPT

ON THIS PAGE

A fictional conversation

This Q&A is an invented editorial example with a fictional father, Daniel. It demonstrates an interview layout; these are not statements from a real interviewee.

We often describe a good man through what he provides. But what happens when provision becomes the only language he knows? In this imagined conversation, Daniel, a father learning to be more present, talks about the work nobody can put on a résumé.

The conversation

When did your idea of being a good man begin to change?

When I realised I could be reliable at work and unreliable emotionally at home. I remembered every deadline but kept postponing difficult conversations with people I loved. I had to stop treating presence as something I would offer after everything else was finished.

What did taking responsibility look like that week?

One apology without a defence attached. I told my brother I had dismissed him. Then I listened. Nothing dramatic happened. We made dinner. It felt small, but I had finally stopped trying to win the conversation.

Where does faith fit into that?

It gives me somewhere to bring the gap between the man I announce and the man I actually am. Reflection matters, but so does repairing what I damaged. For me, a prayer has to make room for a phone call afterwards.

What would you tell someone who feels behind?

Choose one relationship and one promise. Make the promise small enough to keep. You do not need to become an impressive new person by Friday. You need to become a little more trustworthy today.

One question to take away

Who receives the least patient version of you—and what would a more generous response look like this evening?
