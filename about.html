ABOUT MENCLAVE

A life worth
thinking about.

Menclave is an independent, autonomous organisation exploring the art of being a man—with faith, purpose, curiosity and room for honest conversation.

Our purpose

We publish stories and perspectives on work, relationships, culture, belief and the everyday decisions that shape a life. We make space for thoughtful disagreement, the lessons of experience and questions that deserve more than a quick answer.

Made possible by support

Menclave is made possible by the support of donors, including Prosperity Tech, a non-profit organisation, and individual supporters who believe in its mission.

Independent voices

Menclave operates independently and autonomously. The opinions expressed by contributors are their own and do not reflect the opinions of Prosperity Tech or other donors and supporters.
