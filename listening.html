Learning to Listen Across Differences

Curiosity and conviction can share the same conversation.

MENCLAVE · EDITORIAL CONCEPT

← All stories

Begin with a question

Ask what experience shaped the other person’s view. Listen long enough to understand their answer before preparing your reply.

Speak with care

State your convictions clearly while leaving room for correction. You can disagree without turning a person into an argument to win.

Know when to pause

Some conversations need time. A respectful pause can give both people space to return with more patience.
