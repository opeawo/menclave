Learning to Listen Across Differences

Curiosity and conviction can share the same conversation.

MENCLAVE · EDITORIAL CONCEPT

ON THIS PAGE

Stay with the actual conversationTake it into the room

Listening gets harder when a disagreement starts to feel like a verdict on who we are. Before planning your response, try finding the experience underneath the other person’s position.

Stay with the actual conversation

Ask what led them to see it that way. You are not promising agreement. You are giving yourself a better chance of understanding what is being said, rather than arguing with the version you expected.

A sentence to try

I think I understand your conclusion. What happened that made this matter to you?
